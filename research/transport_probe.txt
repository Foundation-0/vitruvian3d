Periodic accessibility during folding: numerical research assessment
gpt-forge. gpt-6.1-sol. 2026-11-12

Call
GO for a narrow geometry/transport follow-up, subject to a prior-art check.
The strongest question is whether equal-density configurations of the same
articulated sphere framework can switch an object's unbounded access between
two and three independent directions. The computations exhibit that effect.
NO claim of a new packing, anatomical law, practical sieve, or new pore-network
algorithm follows. Computing pore thresholds is standard work. A useful paper
would need an exact/robust mechanism-specific theorem or physical design result,
not just the numerical table below.

Model and meaning
Sites are +/-e_i+nL, sphere radius r=1/sqrt(2), with
L=(2+sqrt(2)cos(theta))I+sin(theta)C.
We test both the original fcc route and the skew vacancy-fcc route.
A spherical probe of radius q is equivalent to inflating every obstacle sphere
to radius r+q. Connect periodic Voronoi vertices by their full Voronoi edges.
each edge is weighted by minimum clearance over its ENTIRE straight segment.
Segment/site distances use the analytical clamped projection, not vertex-only
clearance or a sampled segment. The graph retains exact integer cell shifts.
Weighted union-find tracks cell-offset potentials. a nonzero residual around
a cycle detects periodic traversal. Rank means the maximum rank of the integer
winding subgroup WITHIN ONE connected component. Directions from disconnected
components are never pooled. Rank2 means unbounded transport in a lattice plane.
rank3 means three linearly independent unbounded periodic directions. This
does not measure diffusion rates, tortuosity, or dynamic bodily movement.

Matched-density result (critical control)
Original route at theta=40 degrees:
    det L = 31.118701467217385
    largest q/r admitting winding rank2 = 1.12136622761898
    largest q/r admitting winding rank3 = 0.9752400089184668
Skew route at theta=46.11134177121956 degrees, SAME det L and packing fraction:
    largest q/r admitting winding rank3 = 1.0133559623646695
Thus a probe with q/r=1 has rank2 access on the original route and rank3 access
on the skew route at the same occupied volume fraction. This is a shape/direction
effect, not merely extra empty volume. The thresholds at this matched comparison
agree between replication radii 2,3,4 to below1e-7 in absolute probe radius.
The advantage is not universal: original45deg and density-matched skew51.452deg
have rank3 thresholds .916135 and .914292, respectively.

Rooms are not passageways
The fixed octahedron has central room radius/r=sqrt(2)-1=.414214 but its
triangular exit gate has q/r=2/sqrt(3)-1=.154701. A probe between those sizes
fits the room and cannot leave it. The room and gate are unchanged by either
fold because the motif stays fixed. They must not be confused with the larger
external periodic transport network.
At90deg the original fcc and vacancy-fcc routes have IDENTICAL transport
threshold2/sqrt(3)-1 despite the latter having larger vacant rooms. Ordered
vacancies in this particular pattern do not enlarge the limiting traversal
throat. The useful effect is in the intermediate motion, not endpoint density.

Checks
1. Open cubic endpoint agrees with independent square-window geometry:
   q/r=sqrt(4+2sqrt(2))-1=1.6131259297527523.
2. Full fcc endpoint agrees with independent triangular-window geometry:
   q/r=2/sqrt(3)-1=.15470053837925163.
3. All nine primary angles on both routes agree between site-image cubes
   [-2,2]^3 and[-3,3]^3. Additional angles0,45,89,90 agree with[-4,4]^3.
4. A one-degree angle grid evaluates both routes, including rank equality
   controls. Six additional matched-density comparisons are saved.
5. Union-find controls reject contractible cycles and detect rank1,2,3 only
   after independently shifted cycles are added.
6. JSON retains representative bottleneck locations, nearest supporting sites,
   segment parameters and winding bases. At45deg the rank2 limiting point is
   strictly inside a Voronoi segment (parameter .49573), demonstrating why
   vertex-only clearance would overestimate transport.

Limits and decision criterion
All Voronoi construction and periodic vertex deduplication are floating point.
Coordinates are deduplicated at1e-7 and edge-weight batches at1e-9. Replication
stability is a strong numerical check, not a theorem bounding every omitted
image. There is no independent second implementation of the entire network.
The finite periodic graph represents the sphere-obstacle complement through
the standard Voronoi pore-network construction. no separate voxel or homology
calculation was performed. Bars and actuator hardware are omitted. Zero-thickness
contact links lie inside the contacting sphere pair, but finite rods add obstacles.
No manufacturing tolerances, force model, thermal motion, adsorption, flow,
or finite-size sample boundaries are modeled.

Recommended bounded follow-up: independently reproduce the matched-density
rank2/rank3 counterexample. derive exact active throat formulas and certify the
absence of bypasses near40deg. test stability to finite rods and mild motif
asymmetry. If that directional window survives and prior art does not already
cover the same framework, it merits a focused research project. If it disappears
under tiny realistic perturbations, retain it as a geometric illustration rather
than a materials proposal. A broad Vitruvian/anatomical theory remains unsupported.

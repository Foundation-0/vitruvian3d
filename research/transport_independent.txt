Independent periodic-grid transport validation
2026-11-12. gpt-forge. Model: gpt-6.1-sol.

Result
A separate uniform-grid computation finds rank-two transport for the original
fold and rank-three transport for the skew fold at matched density. The skew
rank-three result has explicit, independently rechecked polygonal path witnesses.
No Voronoi geometry or union-find code from transport_probe.py is used.

Geometry
Sphere radius and probe radius are both 1/sqrt(2), so the required separation
between a probe center and any obstacle center is sqrt(2).
Original: theta=40 degrees, C rows(0,-1,-1),(1,0,-1),(1,-1,0).
Skew: theta=46.1113417712 degrees, C rows(0,1,1),(-1,0,1),(-1,-1,0).
The respective cell determinants are 31.118701467217385 and 31.118701467224156.
The density match is approximate to the supplied angle precision.

Independent method
Use m^3 uniformly spaced fractional grid points with half-grid offset, periodic
six-neighbor adjacency, and the given lattice matrix to convert to Euclidean
coordinates. Keep only nodes whose nearest obstacle separation exceeds sqrt(2).
Every accepted edge is certified by either a conservative Lipschitz distance
bound from its endpoints, or the analytic closest-point projection onto the
segment for all possibly intersecting spheres. A KD-tree supplies candidates.

Breadth-first search assigns integer covering-space cell coordinates. A graph
edge inconsistent with these assignments gives an integer periodic winding.
Cross products and scalar triple products select independent winding vectors.
No graph winding means no grid path, which is weaker than no continuum path.

Results by resolution
m=32: original rank 2, skew rank 0.
m=64: original rank 2, skew rank 0.
m=96: original rank 2, skew rank 3.
The smaller grids miss the narrow skew connections. At m=96, all free grid nodes
in each case form one connected component. Original: 60508 free nodes, 171210
accepted edges. Skew: 75602 free nodes, 216750 accepted edges.

Explicit witness routes
Each route starts at a grid node and ends at its indicated lattice translation.
At m=96, reconstruct the cycles using the BFS parent tree. Recheck every segment
by analytic projection against a larger obstacle image set[-3,3]^3. The smallest
separation above the required sqrt(2) along each complete route is:
Original winding(0,0,-1), 180 segments: 0.0005874914162853351.
Original winding(0,-1,0), 180 segments: 0.0005874914162853351.
Skew winding(1,0,0), 172 segments: 0.0005895282241035193.
Skew winding(0,1,0), 198 segments: 0.000002684444420752996.
Skew winding(0,0,1), 188 segments: 0.00015454624544952722.
The JSON retains the full lifted fractional coordinates of every witness route.
These positive margins exceed the 1e-10 acceptance guard substantially, but the
calculation uses floating-point arithmetic and is not a formal interval proof.

Periodic image control
For segments beginning in the fundamental cell, a missing translation outside
[-2,2]^3 has at least one fractional separation >=2-1/m. Therefore its obstacle
center distance is at least sigma_min(L)*(2-1/m)-1. This bound exceeds 4 in both
cases, well above sqrt(2). For the larger-image route recheck, segment starts
are wrapped into the fundamental cell before each projection calculation.

Interpretation and limits
Independent positive paths establish three-dimensional transport for the skew
case at this probe size in the sphere-only model. The grid also constructs two
independent periodic routes for the original case. Its failure to find a third
is consistent with the Voronoi result but does not independently prove an upper
bound of two. Connector thickness, joints, mechanics, and physical dynamics
are absent. This verifies a geometric effect, not its novelty or manufacturability.

Directional compatibility in the exact quarter-circle motion
gpt-forge, gpt-6.1-sol. 2026-11-12

Scope
Enumerate all 64 matrices C with diagonal zero and off-diagonal entries +/-1,
using L(theta)=(2+sqrt(2)cos(theta))I+sin(theta)C, 0<=theta<=pi/2.
The motif is {+/-e_i}. spheres have radius 1/sqrt(2).
Each bridge has length sqrt(2), regardless of C. Equal local reach therefore
does not guarantee globally compatible movement.

Result
Twenty of the 64 coordination choices preserve non-overlap for the whole
continuous motion. Twelve give fcc. Eight give a periodic vacancy pattern
occupying six sevenths of an fcc lattice. The remaining 44 have exact site
coincidences at the endpoint. four also have a singular endpoint lattice.

Classification by cubic signed-permutation conjugation
Here k counts reciprocal pairs Cij=Cji, and T is the sum of the two directed
triangle products C12*C23*C31 and C13*C32*C21.

  k   T   choices   det L(90deg)   result
  3  +2      4          4        overlapping sites
  2   0     24          6        overlapping sites
  1  -2     12          8        overlapping sites
  3  -2      4          0        singular. overlapping sites
  1  +2     12         12        collision-free, fcc, 12 contacts/site
  0   0      8         14        collision-free, vacancy fcc, 10 contacts/site

The 64 matrices partition into exactly these six orbits. For d=2+sqrt(2)cos(theta)
and t=sin(theta), det L=d^3-d*t^2*(2k-3)+t^3*T. T=det C is an orientation-loop
invariant here, not a measure of chirality.

Why this is not chirality
Every full cubic conjugation P C P^T has the same action as (-P) C (-P)^T.
In dimension three one of P and -P is a proper rotation. Consequently full
and proper cubic conjugacy orbits coincide. Moreover, every infinite set
{p+nL} in this model is centrosymmetric because p and -p both belong to the
motif and n ranges over all integers. The model cannot distinguish geometric
enantiomers. Directed labels or a different motif could change that conclusion,
but are additional assumptions.

The unexpected alternative endpoint
A representative of the eight extra feasible choices is the skew matrix
    [ 0  1  1 ]
    [-1  0  1 ]
    [-1 -1  0 ].
Its endpoint lattice L=2I+C has determinant 14 and lies in D3 (even coordinate
sum), which has determinant 2. Thus L has index 7 in D3. The six distinct motif
cosets fill six of the seven cosets in the odd-parity translate of D3.
More explicitly, the endpoint centers are the integer points z with odd
coordinate sum and 5*z1+z2+3*z3 not congruent to zero mod 7.
The motif residues are +/-5,+/-1,+/-3, all six nonzero residues mod 7.
For example, the odd-parity fcc site (1,-1,1) is missing: its residue is 7.
Its density is sqrt(2)*pi/7 = 0.6346975625940523, exactly 6/7 of fcc density.
This endpoint interpretation is a deduction in this probe. no priority claim
or literature search is supplied.

The skew path also increases density strictly. Here det L=d*(d^2+3t^2), and
d/dtheta det L = -3*sin(theta)*[5sqrt(2)+4cos(theta)-sqrt(2)cos(theta)^2],
which is negative on the open interval.

Exact non-overlap certificate and bounds
For each nonsingular-endpoint orbit, S=(C+C^T)/2 is a signed graph on three
vertices. With k=0,1,2 active edges its smallest eigenvalue is respectively
0,-1,-sqrt(2). With k=3 and T=+2 it is -1. The remaining k=3,T=-2 case has
eigenvalue -2 and is rejected by exact endpoint witnesses.
Therefore all non-rejected classes have sigma_min(L)>=2-sqrt(2)>0 uniformly.
For any motif difference v, |v|<=2. All translations with |n|^2>=34 are safe:
  |nL+v| >= (2-sqrt(2))*sqrt(34)-2 > sqrt(2).
Enumerating the 799 integer n with |n|^2<=33 and the 19 motif differences
leaves 15180 nontrivial distance inequalities per representative.
Put s=tan(theta/2). each inequality becomes a quartic over Q(sqrt(2)).
For EACH accepted representative all 75900 degree-four Bernstein coefficients
are nonnegative. The sign checks use integer arithmetic p+q*sqrt(2), not a
floating-point tolerance. This certifies every real theta in the interval,
subject to the written finite-image bound and correctness of the script.
Symmetry transfers each certificate to its whole orbit.

For rejected classes, the JSON contains integer endpoint witnesses with
distance squared exactly zero. A negative Bernstein coefficient alone is
never used as evidence of collision.

Controls and evidence limits
The published original matrix is accepted. Flipping its C12 sign produces
an exact endpoint collision and is rejected. Orbit sizes sum to 64.
An independent direct geometric calculation matches 1280 randomly selected
quartic evaluations across all 64 matrices. max floating residual 6.83e-13.
The one-degree angle scan is discovery/checking only. The accepted continuum
claims rest on integer certificates, not that scan. This is one implementation,
not a proof-assistant formalization or independent specialist review.
Contacts reported for rejected patterns count represented pairs even when
sites coincide, so their contact degrees are not physical coordination numbers.

Conceptual implication and next experiment
The local arms all reach the same distances, yet most coordination rules fail.
One locally indistinguishable choice reaches full close packing. another
reaches an ordered pattern of vacancies. The promising abstraction is global
compatibility of coordinated reach, with a discrete direction rule controlling
the eventual occupancy pattern. It is not handedness selection.
Next test: permit independently timed theta_i(t), ask whether a rejected
shared-angle rule can avoid collision to a useful endpoint, and whether a
skew route can connect to the full-fcc route without overlap. Distinguish
unavoidable endpoint impossibility from obstruction caused only by synchronous
timing. The present 64-choice test does not answer those path-space questions.

## Why the threshold for more research is met

1. **A density-controlled effect with independent positive routes.** The original fold at 40 degrees and skew fold at 46.1113417712 degrees have the same normalized cell volume, about 31.1187014672. For a spherical probe equal in radius to the obstacle spheres, the numerical Voronoi roadmap has component winding ranks two and three respectively.

A separate fractional-grid/BFS method constructs two and three actual polygonal routes with positive sphere clearance. It confirms the positive routes. absence of a third route in the original remains a continuum upper-bound result from the Voronoi method, not independently proved by the grid. This warrants an exact throat and bypass analysis..
2. **An exact family beyond a single animation.** Arbitrary separated phase sequences between 40 and 50 degrees integrate to compatible cell positions with unchanged bars and global non-overlap. The proof covers all permitted sequences and times, including traveling modulations. It is a constructive kinematic family, independently reviewed, with physical dynamics excluded.
3. **A constrained switching obstruction.** The known vacancy endpoint is a strict local minimum of cell volume in the one-cell fixed-motif family. Every nonzero allowed tangent increases volume. A denser endpoint exists, but a path to it in this family must initially dilate. The least necessary dilation and the effect of relaxing the cell restriction are precise open optimization questions.

These outcomes make the next steps testable and potentially useful. The value lies in controlling the routes available to an object and understanding the movements required to change those routes. Abstract Vitruvian thinking supplied this change of question. it has not established a correspondence with human anatomy.

## Results that narrow the recommendation

- The endpoint packing and its removal of one seventh of FCC sites are known. Exact coordinate identification matches type 20 in Koch and Fischer's International Tables chapter. Do not present it as a new packing.
- Centre extraction does not supply a robust universal regularizer. A small genuine facet perturbation can cause a finite jump, and an independently reviewed alternative cycle disproves universal cube–octahedron convergence. Keep the exact dynamics as a limited mathematical note.
- The symmetric point-set family has no geometric chirality. Changing directional signs distinguishes coordination patterns, not enantiomers.
- Human proportion, effort geometry, autonomous waves, mechanical memory, and application performance remain unsupported by these computations.

## Focused next program and decision gates

1. Independently certify the continuum transport upper bound and derive intermediate throat formulas. Identify the exact parameter region where a fixed-size probe changes between three-dimensional and planar periodic access at controlled density.
2. Solve the minimum-dilation transition problem between vacancy and full-FCC states. Begin within the one-cell model, then test larger-cell nonuniform changes. Distinguish an endpoint constraint from an unnecessarily restricted route.
3. Test finite connector geometry and mild shape/angle perturbations. A positive sphere-only result does not establish hardware feasibility. If physical freedom vanishes under realistic connectors, retain the mathematical result and decline the material-development branch.

A publication claim additionally needs a focused full-text prior-art comparison and independent specialist review. These are next-stage obligations, not reasons to withhold the current research allocation decision.

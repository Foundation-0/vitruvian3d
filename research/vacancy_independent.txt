Vacancy interpretation
H=Z^3(2I+C) has index 14 in Z^3. Every H vector has even coordinate sum and residue
5z1+z2+3z3=0mod7. The joint parity/residue kernel has the same index 14, hence
is exactlyH. Six motif cosets occupy the six nonzero residue classes in the
odd-parity fcc translate. Exact inverse membership agrees at 729 integer sites,
as a supplemental implementation check. The 12 fcc nearest-neighbor steps have
mod7 residues1..6 each twice. Every occupied site therefore has 10 occupied
neighbors. every vacancy has 12 occupied neighbors. Filling vacancies gives fcc,
so the endpoint is unsaturated.

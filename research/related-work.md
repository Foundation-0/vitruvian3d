Borcea and Streinu's paper appeared in 2021, DOI `10.1177/1081286520948116`. Its title is *Periodic tilings and auxetic deployments*. Related centre-iteration questions use different maps. Any comparison must keep the precise centering, face treatment, and normalization rules fixed.

## Claims to retain and claims to withhold

The contributions here are explicit constructions, obstructions, and reproducible certificates for the stated source model. Their mathematical validity and their historical originality are separate questions. Written proofs and arithmetic replays address the former within their stated scope. A specialist literature comparison is still needed for the latter.

The known vacancy packing is not a discovery claim. Neither auxetic deployment, moving fronts, nor second-order rigidity should be presented as newly invented here. The strongest candidates for closer comparison are the matched-volume probe-access separation, the particular layer-volume construction, and their finite-connector composition.

No anatomical measurements, biological datasets, or human proportion hypotheses were tested. The Vitruvian interpretation supplied a research question about bodies, surrounding space, and possible motion. It did not supply empirical evidence about the human body.
